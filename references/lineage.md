# Method lineage

Identity strategy spans several traditions whose mechanisms should remain distinct.

## Positioning and competitive context

April Dunford's method begins with the alternatives customers actually consider, then relates differentiated attributes, value, best-fit customers, and market category. This prevents category-first storytelling from floating free of the choice context. Primary writing: <https://aprildunford.substack.com/p/positioning-and-competition>

Use it for product positioning. Do not assume every identity bearer has customers or that market choice exhausts cultural meaning.

## Distinctive assets and memory

The Ehrenberg-Bass tradition distinguishes an asset's uniqueness from its fame and treats recognizability and correct brand attribution as empirical questions. Institute overview: <https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/brands-of-distinction>

Use it to separate personal taste from demonstrated recognition. It governs the evaluation of supplied sensory assets; it does not authorize the language model to invent final assets.

## Cultural branding

Douglas Holt's cultural-branding work treats brands as participants in cultural contradictions and identity myths rather than bundles of functional associations. Author overview: <https://www.culturalstrategygroup.com/>; book chapter: <https://www.aef.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/Chapter_2_holt.pdf>

Use it when an identity's value depends on social meaning, ideology, community, or historical tension. Do not fabricate cultural authority from remote pattern familiarity; situated research remains necessary.

## Marketing semiotics

Semiotic research analyzes the codes through which signs acquire denotative, connotative, categorical, and cultural meaning. A practitioner overview showing applications to identity, positioning, segmentation, and communication: <https://www.marketingsemiotics.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/Semiotica-2015-.pdf>

Use code mapping to articulate likely readings and territories. Avoid pretending there is one universal decoding or one standardized semiotic method.

## The contribution of this skill

The skill joins positioning, cultural interpretation, category codes, behavioral proof, and candidate critique while enforcing an epistemic authorship boundary. It is designed to be strong where language-model training provides real footing—conceptual relations, precedent, narrative, category comparison, and articulated reception hypotheses—and deliberately abstinent where situated sensory judgment and taste are the primary instruments.

This is not a novelty claim over brand strategy. The contribution is the agent behavior: complete conceptual direction without counterfeit sensory authority.
